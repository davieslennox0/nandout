NexusHook: circuit-governed Uniswap v4 fees

FeeRouteHook is a generic v4 hook: any pool on X Layer may use it. Two circuits pick the LP fee tier (0.05 / 0.30 / 0.60 / 1.00%) from volatility and depth measured against each pool's own baseline. Two more pick where a 5 bps route fee goes, and every route is wired to the pool's in-range LPs. Each circuit's full output was computed once at deploy, so a TapeOut upgrade cannot change the fee or its destination.

Hook fee, disclosed: 1000 pips (0.10%) of the swap input in both directions, the same model and cap as Uniswap's own protocol fee. It is paid to the Nandout deploy wallet 0x934d315C0a9C0866D393B722C1805F2B6b20b816, and everything else goes to the pool's LPs. For example, a 1,000-token swap in the 0.30% tier pays 1.000 to the deploy wallet, 2.997 LP fee and a 0.4975 route fee to LPs. No pool is deployed yet: liquidity is capital, not gas.
